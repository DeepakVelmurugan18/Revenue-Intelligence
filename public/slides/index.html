Interface Choices & Deliberately Rejected Decisions

Designing for managerial actionability while guarding against speculative numbers.

📐 Interface Hierarchy & Workflow Design
  • Manager Daily Briefing (21 Aug 2026 Asia/Kolkata): Surfaces P1 escalations, key follow-ups, and coaching highlights at a glance.
  • Mathematical Integrity Cockpit: Exposes explicit denominators (e.g., 18/44 usable coverage; 11/30 stored connection rate). Separates Call Reps from Meeting Organizers.
  • Specialized Action Cards: Differentiates output format by category (Coaching cards, Deal action cards, Bug repro cards).
  • Evidence Inspector & Audio Reader: Turn-by-turn timestamps, click-to-quote citations, and verifiable manual route override audit trails.
  • Interactive Replay Stepper: Step-by-step verification of initial batch, idempotent reload, route override, update batch, and persistence.
🚫 Deliberately Rejected Decision: Synthetic Pipeline Valuation

The Considered Alternative: Synthesizing CRM deal dollar values and win-probability forecasts from conversational currency mentions.

Why Rejected: In M03, Customer B mentions "perhaps INR 26–39 lakh a month" of manual vendor spend. In M10, Team member A mentions an "INR 6,240" subscription deduction. Naive algorithms parse these as SaaS deal values.

Data Contract Warning: "No CRM opportunity table, authoritative deal value, or full account history is supplied. Missing inputs cannot be replaced by guesses."

Accepted Trade-off: We deliberately omit dollar revenue forecasting. Instead, we display qualitative evaluation signals ("High Intent", "Budget Constrained") and verified operational action items.

Slide 5 of 5 • Technical Review & Roadmap

Trade-offs, AI Usage, Limitations & Future Vision

Transparent disclosure of architectural choices, known constraints, and future evolution.

⚖️ Technical Trade-offs

Deterministic Engine vs. Live LLM Calls: Pre-generated deep intelligence for all 44 records guarantees 100% reproducible tests without requiring reviewers to provide paid API keys or face rate limits.

Client-Side Replay Machine: Transactional state machine with localStorage persistence and in-browser ZIP generation allows instant launch with zero installation.

🤖 AI Methodology & Boundaries

Analytical Acceleration: AI was used to cross-examine timelines, extract structured MEDDPICC items, and identify speaker discrepancies (e.g., C06 Meera vs. Ravi).

Zero Hallucination Rule: Every analytical sentence is mapped to an exact passage. Background records (B01–B20, M05/M08/M11–M14) are strictly held in intake status without fabricated summaries.

🚀 Limitations & Production Roadmap

Current Limitations: Single-day extract (21 Aug 2026); anonymized phone tokens prevent geo-calling analysis; no authoritative CRM deal stage table.

Future Roadmap:

  • Live webhook ingestion for Zoom, Gong & Twilio.
  • Bi-directional CRM sync (pushing verified next steps to HubSpot/Salesforce).
  • Direct Jira P1 ticket dispatch from bug transcripts.
